RAPID KL RAIL — FARE STRUCTURE OVERVIEW
Source: Rapid Rail Sdn Bhd official fare tables (myrapid.com.my) and the
Shah Alam Line fare structure announcement (June 2026).

GENERAL FARE PRINCIPLES (all Rapid KL rail lines: LRT, MRT, Monorail, BRT, LRT3)
- Fares are distance-based: the further you travel, the more you pay.
- Three fare structures exist:
  1. CASH / TOKEN fare — the highest price tier, paid at station counters or
     ticket machines.
  2. CASHLESS fare — cheaper than cash. Applies to Touch 'n Go cards,
     concession-free stored value, and open payment (debit/credit cards).
     Cashless is typically around 10-15% cheaper than the cash fare.
  3. CONCESSION fare — roughly HALF the cashless fare. For eligible MyKad
     holders: senior citizens, persons with disabilities (OKU), and students
     with concession cards (e.g. Rapid Emas, student passes).
- The core network is INTEGRATED: transferring between LRT (Kelana Jaya,
  Ampang/Sri Petaling), MRT (Kajang, Putrajaya), Monorail and BRT within the
  paid area is a single continuous fare — you do not pay twice.
- EXCEPTION: the LRT Shah Alam Line (LRT3) is fare-wise separate — it has its
  own published fare table and is not part of the 156-station integrated fare
  matrix. A journey combining LRT3 with the rest of the network (e.g.
  transferring at Bandar Utama) costs the LRT3 fare PLUS the integrated
  network fare. The same payment methods (Touch 'n Go, open payment) are
  accepted on both.
- The minimum fare on the network starts from RM 0.80 (short cashless trips).
- Monthly unlimited passes (My50 at RM50 for 30 days of unlimited Rapid KL
  rail and bus travel) are usually cheaper than daily fares for regular
  commuters. See the My50 FAQ document for eligibility.

LRT SHAH ALAM LINE (LRT3) FARES — VERIFIED HEADLINE FIGURES
- End-to-end, Bandar Utama to Johan Setia (the full 37.8 km line):
  - Cash: RM 4.90
  - Cashless: RM 4.30
  - Concession: RM 2.40
- Shortest trips (one station, e.g. Bandar Utama to Kayu Ara):
  - Cash: RM 1.20 or less
  - Cashless: from RM 0.80-1.10
  - Concession: from RM 0.40-0.60
- Example mid-distance trip, Bandar Utama to Stadium Shah Alam:
  approximately RM 3.70 cash / RM 3.20 cashless / RM 1.80 concession.
- LRT3 accepts the same payment media as the rest of the network: Touch 'n Go,
  open payment, concession passes, and the My50 monthly pass are all valid.

HOW TO GET AN EXACT FARE
- Exact station-to-station fares are available in this knowledge base:
  the RAPID KL INTEGRATED FARE TABLE document (all 156 integrated-network
  stations, cash and cashless) and the LRT3 SHAH ALAM LINE FARE TABLE
  document (all 20 LRT3 stations, cash, cashless and concession).
- The same fares are also published in the official fare calculator at
  myrapid.com.my (Rapid KL > Integrated Fare Table) and the MyRapid PULSE
  app.
