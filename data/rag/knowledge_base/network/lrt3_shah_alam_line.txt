LRT SHAH ALAM LINE (LRT3) — LINE INFORMATION
Source: official GTFS feed (data.gov.my, July 2026) and Rapid KL (myrapid.com.my).

The LRT Shah Alam Line, also known as LRT3, opened for passenger service on
29 June 2026. It runs 37.8 km from Bandar Utama in Petaling Jaya to Johan Setia
in Klang, serving the Shah Alam and Klang corridor.

The line currently has 20 operational stations (five additional
in-fill stations are planned to open around 2028: Tropicana, Temasya, Raja Muda,
Bukit Raja Selatan, and Bandar Botanik).

Interchanges:
- Bandar Utama: connection to the MRT Kajang Line. This is an out-of-station
  transfer — passengers exit, walk at street level, and re-enter (allow around
  10-12 minutes).
- Glenmarie 2: connection to the LRT Kelana Jaya Line at Glenmarie station via
  a direct pedestrian link bridge (allow around 8 minutes).
- At Klang, a walking link connects toward the KTM Klang station.

The line accepts the same payment media as other Rapid KL lines — Touch 'n Go,
open payment, and passes such as My50 — but its fares are published in a
separate LRT3 fare table: it is not part of the 156-station integrated fare
matrix. A journey combining LRT3 with another line (e.g. transferring at
Bandar Utama) costs the LRT3 fare plus the integrated network fare.
